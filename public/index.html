The process

A personal problem.
A working platform.

I was spending time on English without making the progress I wanted. I began researching memory and long-term learning, then made that question the starting point of my school project.

Over six months, I took Eaglish from the first idea to a working application. I built the project from beginning to end; today, my friends and I use it regularly.

Over the same period, my self-assessed English level improved from A2 to B2. Building the platform also changed the way I approach learning.
