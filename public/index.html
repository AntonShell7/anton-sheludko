01 / PERSONAL PROJECTLive & in use

Eaglish.

An English-learning platform

I was spending time on English without making the progress I wanted. That question became my school project: how could I build a better way to learn?

The idea

I began researching memory and long-term learning. Over six months, I turned that research into a working platform built around vocabulary in context and spaced repetition.

What I built

I took Eaglish from the initial idea to the working application. It brings together reading, vocabulary practice, and writing. My friends and I now use it regularly.

What changed for me

Building Eaglish changed how I approach learning languages. Over the same six months, my self-assessed English level improved from A2 to B2.

Built with
  • React
  • TypeScript
  • Vite
  • Tailwind CSS
  • Supabase
