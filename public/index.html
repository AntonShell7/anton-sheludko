02 / Something I built

One problem.
A project of my own.

From a school assignment
to part of my everyday life.

Live & in use

Eaglish.

A better way to practise English.
Built because I needed one.

I was putting time into English without seeing the progress I wanted. I turned that frustration into my school project — and spent six months taking it from an idea to a working platform.

Today, my friends and I use it regularly for reading, vocabulary practice, and writing.

The question

How can learning last?

I researched memory and long-term learning, then explored vocabulary in context and spaced repetition.

My contribution

My self-assessed English level improved over the same period. The project also changed how I approach studying.
